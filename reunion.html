The Kiefer Family Reunion

Dr. Herman Kiefer arrived in Detroit in 1849 with a sword, a diploma and a small bag of gold coins. Today his descendants are scattered from Michigan to Sweden, and most of us have never met. We're planning a family reunion in Detroit: a celebration of the family, a look at where it all began, and a chance to finally put faces to names.

Every living descendant of Herman and Frances Kiefer is welcome, through any branch and under any last name: Kiefer, Danziger, Parker, Blackburn, Heym, Orr, Nilsson, Jones, Bonning, Marr, Lawrie, Owen, Bayliss, Bajis, Gilbert and more.
