Open Questions

Family history is detective work. These are the questions we haven't answered yet. If you have a record, a photo or a memory that helps, please share it.

Open question

Two mothers in the records

The Saga and his death certificate name Christine Schweykert; the 1825 Sulzburg baptism register names Rosina Sophie Grether.

Open question

Chloroform or ether?

The Saga says Herman was put under chloroform at Heidelberg on a dare; other accounts say ether, at Freiburg.

Open question

Woodmere or Elmwood?

Find a Grave lists Dr. Kiefer at Woodmere Cemetery; the Saga places the family monument at Elmwood.

Open question

Arthur and Kate's divorce

Arthur married Olga Gagel in 1923/24 while Kate lived until 1945. The family believes they divorced; we haven't found the record.

Open question

When did Marie Aubert Kiefer die?

Edwin's widow lived in France through the occupation and saw American troops liberate Paris in 1944. Her exact death date is unknown.

Open question

Where is "The Letter"?

Edwin's prize-winning Paris Salon painting of 1890 has not been located.
