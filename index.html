The documentary · 21 minutes

Kiefer: Do Right and Fear No One

The story in one sentence

A revolutionary who became a pillar of Detroit — and whose son refused to have a hospital named after himself.

For a hundred years, most people knew only the name on the building. In 1951 his grandson wrote the whole story down and left blank pages at the back for the next generation. This website is that next volume.

It's about all of them

The Six Children

Nine were born; six grew up. Each went in a different direction, and each left descendants we are still finding.

The engineer · 1852–1909

Alfred Kossuth

Mining engineer, banker and paper-mill founder. Fought eleven student duels in Germany.

The keeper · 1855–1932

Arthur Egmont

Engineer who saved his father's papers — and inherited Napoleon's dueling pistols.

The artist · 1860–1931

Edwin Hans

Won a prize at the 1890 Paris Salon, then married the woman in the painting.

The tanner · 1862–1927

Edgar Siegfried

Built the world's largest pigskin tannery, in Grand Rapids and Chicago.

The daughter · 1864–1938

Hermina Cora

Married her father's successor; the old doctor spent his last year in her home.

The health officer · 1867–1930

Guy Lincoln

Built Detroit's health department and named its hospital for his father.
