Are you a Kiefer relative?

Our privacy promise

Living relatives stay private. The public family tree shows living people only as "Living relative (private)" unless they ask to be listed.

You choose who sees what you share. Everyone, family only, or only Jim. Nothing appears until Jim has reviewed it.

Your contact details are never published. Email addresses and phone numbers are seen only by Jim.

Family is not marketing. Joining the family or sharing a photo never signs you up for business updates. That is a separate box you can tick — or not.

Change your mind any time. Write to Jim and he will update or remove anything you shared.
