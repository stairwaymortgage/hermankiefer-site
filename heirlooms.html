Heirlooms

In 1951 the Saga recorded treasures held by different branches of the family. Seventy-five years later, we're trying to find them. Is one of these in your attic?

Last known: 1951 — with Edgar W. Kiefer, Port Huron

Napoleon's gold snuff box

Said to be a gift from Napoleon to the Elector of Baden, passed to Dr. Ludwig Kiefer.

Last known: 1951 — Arthur's family

Napoleon's dueling pistols, marked "N"

Left to Arthur Kiefer by his friend Prof. Alexander Ziwet of the University of Michigan.

Last known: 1951 — in the family; holder not named

Bruno Mauch's revolver

Carried by 1st Lt. Bruno Mauch, 1st Michigan Infantry, who died in Libby Prison.

Last known: 1951 — Dr. Guy Kiefer's family

Frances Kiefer's diamond ring

Bought in Europe in 1873; later given to her son Guy.

Last known: 1951 — in the family

The golden-wedding mug

Made for Dr. Conrad Kiefer's golden wedding anniversary, 1877.

Last known: 1951 — in the family

Oil portraits of Dr. Conrad and Christine Kiefer

Herman's parents, painted in Germany.

Last known: Unknown

"The Letter" by Edwin Kiefer

Marie Aubert in a red blouse; prize at the Paris Salon, 1890.

Last known: University of Michigan? — not yet confirmed

Life-size portrait of Dr. Herman Kiefer

Painted by his son Edwin for the University of Michigan medical school faculty room.

Last known: Unknown

"The George Washington picture"

Edwin's oil portrait of his mother, Frances.

Last known: Unknown

Dr. Kiefer's sword and diploma

What he carried off the ship in 1849.
