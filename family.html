Herman & Frances Kiefer's children

The Family

Nine children were born between 1851 and 1867. Emila Anna, Richard Faust and Oscar Hutton died young and lie at Elmwood Cemetery. The other six went out into the world.

The engineer · 1852–1909

Alfred Kossuth

Mining engineer, banker and paper-mill founder. Fought eleven student duels in Germany.

The keeper · 1855–1932

Arthur Egmont

Engineer who saved his father's papers — and inherited Napoleon's dueling pistols.

The artist · 1860–1931

Edwin Hans

Won a prize at the 1890 Paris Salon, then married the woman in the painting.

The tanner · 1862–1927

Edgar Siegfried

Built the world's largest pigskin tannery, in Grand Rapids and Chicago.

The daughter · 1864–1938

Hermina Cora

Married her father's successor; the old doctor spent his last year in her home.

The health officer · 1867–1930

Guy Lincoln

Built Detroit's health department and named its hospital for his father.

Before Detroit

The ancestors

The Saga traces the family back to a Dutch knight around 1650 and a line of physicians and officials in Baden. Much of it is family tradition we are still working to verify.
